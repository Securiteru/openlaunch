## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/aiagentsdirectory/), observed unknown.

AI Agents Directory, self-styled DIRA, lists AI agents and frameworks by category with a leaderboard, daily agent news, a newsletter and a comparison arena. A listing page carries overview, features, use cases, pricing model, alternatives, a review block and quick facts such as category, access and listing date.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

Teams shipping an agent or agent tooling that fits one of the directory’s categories (coding, sales, marketing, voice, tool libraries and so on); the about page positions the site as infrastructure for an agent economy rather than a general software list.

### What to prepare

An account — the submit page is client-rendered and its sign-in gate appears before the form.
A one-line pitch, a longer overview, three to five features and use cases, pricing model and access type, since that is what a published page renders.
A decision on paid placement: the free tier asks for a badge on your site and gives a nofollow link, while dofollow, featured spots and ecosystem partner slots are paid.

### What “live” means here

Live means your agent resolves under /agent/<slug>/ with a “Listed” date in the quick facts and a visit button; new entries carry a NEW label and start at zero reviews.

### Worth knowing

The public listing is heavy on generated evaluation text and a “buzz” percentage; check that the features and pricing shown match your product and edit if not. Some outbound links on the site are affiliate links, which the footer states openly.
