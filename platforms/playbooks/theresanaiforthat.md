## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/theresanaiforthat/), observed unknown.

There’s An AI For That (TAAFT) is a large AI tool aggregator that calls itself “the front page of AI”; its pages show a live counter of the day’s tool searches and the site says more than 90 million people have used it. Listings sit in a database you browse by task, alongside leaderboards, collections and a newsletter the site puts at 2.5M+ subscribers, sent three times a week.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

AI tool makers with a finished product and a budget: submission is paid and everything is reviewed by staff. The page says it accepts AI tools, GPTs and non-human AI influencers.

### What to prepare

The tool URL — the launch page says your tool page is populated from it, with edit access after you verify your email.
Payment: the page shows $49 for the directory listing and $437 for the package that adds a newsletter slot; the FAQ calls the fee one-off and automatically refunded if editorial does not publish the tool.
A landing page that can absorb a traffic spike; the FAQ tells submitters to check the page and their payment flow before launching.

### What “live” means here

Live means your tool has its own page under /ai/ and turns up in searches and task lists; the FAQ puts processing at one to two days on average and promises an email when it goes online. Listings do not expire and later edits are free.

### Worth knowing

A free route exists, but a narrow one: the FAQ describes a monthly X thread where indie makers can submit and one tool per thread gets listed at no cost. Extras are separate products — a $99 per month highlight and a pay-per-click Featured section where your bid sets the position. The traffic and click figures on the page are the platform’s own marketing.
