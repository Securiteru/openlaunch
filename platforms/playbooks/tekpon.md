## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/tekpon/), observed unknown.

Tekpon is a software marketplace and media platform founded in 2021, with category pages across AI, analytics, operations, marketing, security and more, user reviews, a magazine, a podcast and a procurement service. Its get-listed page sells a product profile for a one-time fee of $249.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

B2B SaaS vendors who sell to business buyers and want a reviewed marketplace profile in a defined category; the software catalogue invites buyers to browse over 6,000 software and AI products.

### What to prepare

A short description (max 300 words), a “best for” statement, recommended company sizes, FAQs and details on contact, pricing, features and integrations — the fields the get-listed page enumerates.
The $249 one-time payment for the profile, and a category choice; extra categories and sponsorships belong to the Premium, Gold and Platinum seller tiers that are sold by booking a call.
Customers willing to review: the marketplace is review-driven and the seller tiers add review-based features such as an AI review summary.

### What “live” means here

Live means your product profile resolves in its category with the website link working. Seller badges, verified status and sponsored placement arrive with the paid tiers, not with the basic listing.

### Worth knowing

Tekpon also runs procurement and awards programmes; those are separate businesses on the same domain. Seller tier prices are not published on the pricing page — write “not published” rather than an estimate.
