## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/omr/), observed unknown.

OMR Reviews is the DACH-native B2B software review platform: products and agencies with verified, mostly German-language reviews, star ratings and quarterly badges such as Leader or Top 100, organised in categories from CRM to AI agents.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

Software vendors with German-speaking customers who can collect real reviews — a listing without reviews has no badge and little visibility.

### What to prepare

Company and product information — the vendor page says the team needs specific information to list a product but does not render the fields publicly.
A plan to invite German-speaking customers to review; badges are computed quarterly from reviews.
Awareness of the paid formats named on the vendor page (pay-per-click placements, buyer intent data, webinars, sponsored content); the basic listing is described as free.

### What “live” means here

The site says you will be notified by email with a confirmation that the product has been successfully listed. Live is that email plus a resolving product profile with logo and review counter.

### Worth knowing

Use the English path /en/reviews to read the vendor pages; the root domain redirects to the German site. The platform emphasises German-language verified reviews, so work out who can write one before you list.
