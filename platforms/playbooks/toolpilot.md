## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/toolpilot/), observed unknown.

ToolPilot is an AI tools directory and review platform organised in more than twenty categories — text, image, video, coding, chatbots and more. Each product page shows vendor, description, price, star rating and badges such as Editor’s Pick or Free Trial.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

Makers of AI products who want a category listing with a review section, and who can place a ToolPilot badge or logo on their site to qualify for the free tier.

### What to prepare

The submit page states that a link back to ToolPilot is mandatory for all free listings and must be confirmed via URL or email after adding one of their badges.
Product details as shown on existing pages: name, category, description, pricing model.
A look at the pricing table, which is published as an image on the submit page; the numbers are not readable as text.

### What “live” means here

Live means a page under /products/ that shows up in its category with the rating widget. The public pages show no review timeline, so check for the page directly.

### Worth knowing

A login link exists in the header, but the submit page itself does not say an account is required; be ready for either.
