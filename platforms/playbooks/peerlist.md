## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/peerlist/), observed unknown.

Peerlist is a professional network for developers and designers built around proof-of-work profiles, a feed called Scroll, startup jobs and Launchpad. Launchpad lets you launch projects on Monday every week to find the first hundred users who give feedback.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

Builders who already keep a Peerlist profile or want one; the launch is tied to the person, so a bare product without a maker profile does not fit.

### What to prepare

A Peerlist profile with a claimed username — the site checks availability on the homepage.
A project entry with your product’s link and description, attached to your profile.
A Monday: Launchpad launches run weekly, and the Launchpad pages themselves are behind a bot check, so a logged-in browser session is the only practical route.

### What “live” means here

Live means your project appears in that week’s Launchpad list and on your profile. Before the Monday it is scheduled; the exact ranking mechanics are not published on the public pages.

### Worth knowing

The homepage advertises Peerlist Ads separately; the Launchpad launch itself carries no visible price. Because the network is built around proof of work, a profile with real projects on it is worth more here than a polished submission.
