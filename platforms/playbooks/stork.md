## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/stork/), observed unknown.

Stork.AI is an AI tools directory whose submit page promises listings discoverable by coding agents in Cursor, Claude Code, VS Code, Windsurf and Zed. You paste a live site, GitHub repo or npm package and the platform drafts the listing itself, in eight languages with structured data.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

Developer-tool and AI-tool makers who care about being found by agents and MCP clients, not only by people browsing a grid.

### What to prepare

One URL — a live site, repository or package — and time to review and correct the generated copy and logo.
A choice between the one-time fee shown at $49 or placing the Stork badge on your site, which the page says waives the fee entirely.
Awareness that the homepage currently promotes a separate paid news service (Stork Wire); the directory is under /browse and /submit.

### What “live” means here

Live means a page at stork.ai/en/<your-tool> and a card in the browse grid, which is ordered newest first. The submit page says listings go live within the hour once you have picked a path.

### Worth knowing

The site states nobody can buy a spot in the browse grid and featured slots are labelled separately — useful when comparing with pay-to-rank boards.
