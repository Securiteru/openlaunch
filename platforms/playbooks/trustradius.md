## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/trustradius/), observed unknown.

TrustRadius is a B2B technology review site, now part of HG Insights. Its vendor site offers a free product profile and paid plans; the pricing link now redirects to HG Insights, which says TrustRadius is now HG Customer Voice and lists Free, Customer Voice Package and Customer Voice Premium without published prices.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

B2B technology vendors who can get customers to write long reviews — the vendor site says TrustRadius reviews run to 400+ words, and the buyer site says every reviewer is verified.

### What to prepare

A claim of your product profile through the free route on the claim-your-profile page.
Profile content the vendor page lists: product videos or demos, features and integrations, pricing and security information.
A review plan: Top Rated needs 10+ new or updated reviews in the past 12 months, at least 0.5 percent of category traffic, four stars or more and a trScore of 7.5 or higher.

### What “live” means here

Live means your product page resolves under trustradius.com/products/<slug> and appears in its category. Awards and badges only follow once reviews meet the published criteria.

### Worth knowing

The Trusted Seller programme asks for at least 10 reviews a year and disclosure of how you source reviews and use incentives, so plan honest outreach from day one. The main www.trustradius.com site blocks simple scripted requests; open it in a regular browser.
