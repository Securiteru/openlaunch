## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/aitoolhunt/), observed unknown.

AIToolHunt is an AI tools directory with category and tag pages, a “latest tools” feed, community favourites ranked by likes, an editorial blog and sponsored slots on the homepage. Each product gets a profile page with FAQ content, related tools and a website link.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

AI product makers who want a permanent profile in a categorised directory and can choose between a badge-verified free listing and a small one-time payment.

### What to prepare

An account: /submit redirects to login.
Name, tagline, description, categories and tags, and FAQ answers for the profile.
A tier from the pricing page: Free requires the badge on your homepage and is reviewed within 30 days; Basic at $15 publishes instantly without a badge; Pro (shown discounted to $19) adds 15 days of homepage featuring; Sponsor (shown at $39) adds sponsor cards for 15 days.

### What “live” means here

Live means your profile resolves under /item/<slug>/ and appears in search, category and tag pages. A free submission is pending until the review passes; the pricing page says paid plans apply benefits after checkout verification.

### Worth knowing

The pricing page explicitly marks the Sponsor tier’s complimentary guest post as a possible extra, not a promise. Like most directories here it advertises backlink counts and a domain rating; check the actual link on your live page instead of copying the claim.
