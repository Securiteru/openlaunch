## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/launchigniter/), observed unknown.

LaunchIgniter is a launch platform where a product is featured on the homepage for seven days and competes on upvotes for a weekly Winner badge. Products carry category tags and a vote count, and a separate weekly-winners page collects each week’s top three.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

Makers who want a scheduled one-week launch with upvotes and are willing to either wait for a reviewed free slot or pay a small one-time fee for a fixed date.

### What to prepare

A Google sign-in — the login page carries Google OAuth only — and a completed maker profile; the submit form itself renders only for signed-in users.
Logo, screenshots, optional demo video and a launch week.
A tier: the free launch requires the badge, is reviewed manually and gets no fixed date; Basic is shown at $12 and Pro at $15 one-time, both with a fixed date and no review queue.

### What “live” means here

Live means your product is on the homepage during its launch week with a working vote counter. Before that week it is scheduled; the pricing FAQ says each product can be launched only once.

### Worth knowing

The FAQ says the badge checker can be blocked by Cloudflare — there is a manual confirmation box for that case. Pro bundles listings on StartupTrusted and SaaSGrow; the pricing page also lists free badge-based directories you can add while the launch is live.
