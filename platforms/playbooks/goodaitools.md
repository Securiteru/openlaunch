## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/goodaitools/), observed unknown.

Good AI Tools is an AI tools directory organised into dozens of categories — from AI assistants and APIs to directories and launch platforms — with pricing labels (free, freemium, paid, free trial), a featured block and a “latest AI tools” feed. Each entry has its own page under /ai/ with a visit link.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

Makers of AI tools and adjacent software who want a categorised catalog entry; the category list is broad enough to cover dev tools, directories and business software as well.

### What to prepare

A Google login — the get-started page asks you to sign in with Google or create an account before submitting.
Name, one-line description, category and pricing label, which is what every card and entry page shows.
A check of the category list first, so the entry lands where similar tools already sit.

### What “live” means here

Live means your tool resolves under /ai/<slug>/ and appears in its category and the latest feed. No pricing page resolves publicly (/pricing 404s), so any fee or review time is “not published” until you see it after login.

### Worth knowing

The homepage carries a sponsor banner for a third-party submission service; that is advertising, not part of the directory. Keep the one-line description literal — the cards truncate longer text.
