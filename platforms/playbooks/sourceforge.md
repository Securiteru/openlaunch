## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/sourceforge/), observed unknown.

SourceForge has two tracks: open-source project hosting with downloads, and a business software directory of more than 120,000 titles with ratings, comparisons and category rankings. Commercial products use the vendor route.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

Companies selling software to businesses that want a comparison-site profile with reviews; open-source projects use the separate Create a Project route instead.

### What to prepare

The vendor form asks for your name, email, company, title, website, phone, founding year, location, software title, a square hi-res logo, a product description and the target audience, plus a consent checkbox.
A product description written for buyers comparing tools, since pages show ratings, alternatives and Visit Website buttons.
Awareness that upgraded listings and premium packages exist with a separate pricing page; basic listings rank below upgraded ones.

### What “live” means here

The form wording is a request for a business listings page; the pages show no review timeline. Live means a resolving product page with a working Visit Website link and a category placement.

### Worth knowing

Leader and Top Performer badges are earned through collected reviews, not granted at listing time, so plan review collection separately. Open-source projects belong on the separate Create a Project route, which is a different product from a business listing.
