## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/e27/), observed unknown.

e27, operated by Optimatic Pte Ltd, covers Asia’s startup and tech ecosystem with news, contributed articles, funding-news submissions, a job board, events including its Echelon conference, and databases of startups and investors. The startup list filters by location — Singapore, Indonesia, Malaysia, India, Australia, Hong Kong and more — by vertical, and by whether a company is currently raising.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

Companies building in Southeast Asia or selling into it, especially teams in a fundraise: round details are a core part of the profile and of the search filters.

### What to prepare

An e27 account: “Add Company Profile” sits in the signed-in profile menu and points to e27.co/startup/create/profile, a route that met us with a bot check while logged out.
The fields a profile displays: logo, an About text, date founded, website, where you are based, verticals and areas of operation.
Team members, funding rounds and, if you are raising, your fundraising details, which both the profile and the startup filters surface.

### What “live” means here

Live means your page resolves under e27.co/startups/<slug>/ with its About, Team, Fundings and recent news sections. Pages nobody has taken over show a “Claim This Startup Profile” button instead.

### Worth knowing

The claim dialog says e27 reviews a claimed profile and lets you know once it is approved, but no timeframe is published, and the help centre (help.e27.co) would not open on the day we checked — its certificate failed. Many e27 pages also sit behind a bot check that answers 403 to plain HTTP clients, so use a normal browser, and refresh the profile whenever a round closes, because funding news links back to it.
