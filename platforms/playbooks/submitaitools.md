## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/submitaitools/), observed unknown.

Submit AI Tools is an AI directory that groups tools into categories such as productivity, education, marketing, code and IT, image, video and GitHub repos. The homepage shows a featured block, a “recent paid tools” row and per-category lists. Both the submit and the promote route open with a human verification step — click the named colour — not with an account gate.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

AI tool makers who can place a verification link on their own site and want a categorised entry, with the option to pay later for a faster or featured placement.

### What to prepare

A backlink for the free tier: the submit page asks you to add the verification link, verify it from the dashboard and keep it — the listing stays live while the backlink remains active.
Name, a category and a description; the about page says the team reviews each tool and updates details.
A budget decision: the submit page publishes Basic at $19 lifetime, Pro at $99 for 30 days and Spotlight at $999 for 30 days, with 20% off when you pay in crypto.

### What “live” means here

Live means your tool has its own page and appears in its category list. Free submissions are reviewed within five business days according to the page, Basic is published within two days; record pending until the page resolves.

### Worth knowing

Promotion runs by email — the promote page carries no form and no prices, only a contact address. Cards also appear on a Telegram channel the site links from its header.
