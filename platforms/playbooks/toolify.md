## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/toolify/), observed unknown.

Toolify.ai is a large multilingual AI tool directory — the homepage counts 30,778 AIs in 459 categories and the site is published in ten languages. Around the listings it runs rankings by traffic and revenue, a GPT store, an ad library and blog content, and it sells pay-per-click advertising in blocks from 500 clicks upwards.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

AI product owners who want a paid, permanent entry translated into the site’s languages; the submit page is a checkout, so there is no free queue to join.

### What to prepare

Tool name and website URL, plus a choice between letting Toolify generate the listing text and translations or writing the English copy yourself for it to translate.
$99: the submit page and FAQ describe a one-time payment, listing within 48 hours with no queue and a full refund if the tool is not approved — no AI in the product, or explicit adult content, are the reasons they name.
A stable domain, because the entry is meant to last: the FAQ says a published listing never expires.

### What “live” means here

Live means your tool has a page under /tool/ and appears in the listing and the Just Launched section within 48 hours, with a border highlight on the cards. The submit page states that your site gets no fewer than six dofollow links from Toolify; that is Toolify’s own claim, so check the rendered links yourself.

### Worth knowing

The FAQ says plainly that the listing promises no sales or traffic, and that extra clicks and exposure are bought as advertising. The visit, page-view and subscriber figures on those pages are the site’s own numbers.
