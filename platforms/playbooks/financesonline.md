## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/financesonline/), observed unknown.

FinancesOnline is a B2B and SaaS software review platform with category rankings, comparisons and a research centre. Its add-product page cites 2.5 million+ monthly readers, 20,000+ ratings and reviews and 14,000+ listed vendors. Products are scored with its SmartScore on a 1–10 scale, weighted by main functionality, collaboration, customisation, integrations and more.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

SaaS and B2B software vendors, especially those considering paid promotion: the listing form asks for a monthly advertising budget, and the consent text says your data is used to share an offer of their services.

### What to prepare

Your name, job title and email, plus the product name and website URL for the Create a Listing form on the add-product page.
The best-fitting category from the long dropdown, a Skype ID and your time zone for a follow-up.
An honest answer to the budget question, which ranges from “None” to “Over $10,000” a month; no listing price is published.

### What “live” means here

Live means your product gets its own review page under reviews.financesonline.com/p/<slug>/ and appears in a financesonline.com/c/ category. The public pages do not say how long review takes or whether a basic listing is free.

### Worth knowing

Besides user reviews, the scoring page describes a Customer Satisfaction Algorithm that gathers opinions from social media, blogs and other review sites, so reviews elsewhere count too. Do not assume a free listing; ask in writing what is included before you commit a budget.
