## Field observations
Source: LaunchRepo public field guide (https://launchrepo.dev/directories/sideprojectors/), observed unknown.

SideProjectors is a marketplace where developers sell, buy and showcase side projects and look for co-founders; it calls itself the #1 digital product marketplace and says selling is free with zero fees. Projects are filtered by status (for sale, seeking co-founder, showcase, sold), type, price range, monthly revenue and target market.

Checked on September 17, 2026 on the platform’s public pages.

### Who lists here

Makers who want feedback and early users for a live project rather than a launch-day ranking. The showcase status is explicitly for that: the help page says you submit through the normal form and mark the project as showcase instead of for sale.

### What to prepare

An account — the submit route redirects to login, and the login offers Google, GitHub, GitLab, Product Hunt and LinkedIn.
A detailed listing in English: description, screenshots, a working URL and, where you have them, revenue, traffic or user numbers; the seller guidelines say listings with poor grammar, missing detail or low-quality images get rejected.
Patience: every project is reviewed manually, the post-submit screen says approval typically takes 2–3 business days, and a paid priority review moves you to the front of the queue.

### What “live” means here

Live means your project has a public page under /project/ with the SHOWCASE label and appears in the listings. A submitted form is only in the moderation queue; record it as pending until the page resolves.

### Worth knowing

The marketplace side comes with rules — no concepts, templates or reskins — and the seller page states SideProjectors is a platform only, not a party to any deal. Showcase projects can later be flagged as open to sell, which the current listings use as a combined label.
