## Descriptions — pre-approved copy the agent may paste verbatim

**10 words** (startups.gallery, 10words):
> The GitHub of machine learning — models, datasets, demos.

**50 words:**
> Hugging Face is the open platform where the machine learning community
> collaborates on models, datasets and applications. Host and share work,
> discover state-of-the-art research, and deploy models to production —
> free to start, with paid plans for teams and enterprises.

**150 words:**
> Hugging Face is an open platform and community for machine learning.
> The Hub hosts over a million public models, datasets and interactive
> demos (Spaces), from text and image generation to audio, video and
> robotics. Teams use it to share research, discover reusable work, and
> ship ML features — with libraries like Transformers, Diffusers and
> Datasets, plus managed inference and deployment tools. A free tier
> covers public work; paid plans add private collaboration, SSO, audit
> logs and enterprise controls. Founded in 2016, the company is
> headquartered in New York with a distributed team.

**300 words:**
> Hugging Face is the collaboration platform for the machine learning
> community. What started in 2016 as a chatbot app became the de facto
> home for open ML: the Hub now hosts more than a million public model
> repositories, datasets, and Spaces — interactive demos anyone can try
> in the browser.
>
> For researchers and engineers, it is both a publishing venue and a
> toolkit. The open-source libraries — Transformers, Diffusers, Datasets,
> Tokenizers and others — are how most teams actually run modern models.
> Model cards, dataset cards and evaluation metadata make work
> discoverable and reproducible.
>
> For companies, paid tiers add the operational layer: private hubs,
> role-based access, SSO, audit logs, managed inference endpoints and
> dedicated support, with enterprise plans for regulated environments.
>
> The platform is free for public work and openly community-driven —
> models from independent researchers sit next to releases from major
> labs. Headquarters is New York; the team is distributed internationally.
